Understanding the Crisis

A Nation in Need

The ongoing conflict has devastated communities across Iran, displacing millions and leaving families without access to basic necessities.

Infrastructure Destroyed

Over 60% of critical infrastructure including hospitals, schools, and water systems have been damaged or destroyed.

Food Insecurity

3.8 million people face severe food insecurity. Children are the most vulnerable, with malnutrition rates climbing rapidly.

Mass Displacement

4.2 million people have been forced from their homes. Many live in makeshift camps with inadequate sanitation and shelter.

Medical Emergency

Hospitals overwhelmed and undersupplied. Thousands lack access to essential medicines and surgical care.
